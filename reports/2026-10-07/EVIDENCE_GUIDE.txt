AI-assisted audits. Earlier unresolved or rejected claims are superseded
where the main report says so. A filename containing DRAFT or CONDITIONAL
is not alone a current status determination. These are research records,
not externally peer-reviewed certificates.

publication_audits/: dated literature, source and proof checks.

and public-copy SHA256, and any incidental path/usage cleanup. Mathematical
text is preserved. Original workspace files were not modified.

OpenAI repository linked by the report. The imported raw conversation,
account usage and mutable goal/checkpoint data are not published.
