VERDICT

1. OVERVIEW: CHECKS PASSED

- PP is correctly distinguished from the assertion that each specified
  surjection splits. The latter is AC and is not substituted for PP.
- PP => AC_WO => DC agrees with the saved primary-source reconciliation
  AC_WO_DC_PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.txt. The second implication is
  confirmed there against Jech's Theorem 8.2, not an old HTML implication
  matrix. The earlier conflicting search result must remain superseded.
- The definition of w(X) as an attained largest well-ordered cardinal
  under AC_WO is legitimate. Ordinal-indexed choice assembles injections
  of all ordinals below h(X); if h(X) were a limit cardinal, their
  well-orderable union would contradict Hartogs. This also explains why
  the existence of w(X) is not being asserted in bare ZF.
- The original ground parameter mu and its internal omega_2 identification
  are distinguished; this avoids treating the ground continuum as an
  automatically preserved cardinal.
- The W rows correctly include the normal-form consequences FB, NDS and
  finite cardinal antichains, with unbounded finite antichain sizes. They
  do not assert a fixed finite upper bound on all antichain sizes.
- The universal exponent collapse is explicitly a W theorem. Its precise
  all-Y formulation, including trivial bases, matches
  EXACT_EXPONENTIALS_AND_UNIVERSAL_EXPONENT_COLLAPSE_AUDIT.txt. It is not
  inferred merely from the cardinal normal form or transferred to N.
- Choice for arbitrary families of countable MEMBERS has a completed
  second check: COUNTABLE_MEMBER_CHOICE_SECOND_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.txt.
  The front table distinguishes member size from index-set size. It does
  not transfer this theorem from W to N.
- The text distinguishes OP, OEP and BPI, and distinguishes a native
  order-extension witness from the corresponding global principles.
- The first-order relative-consistency bridge remains an explicit
  dependency on Section 8 of the pinned OpenAI source. No transitive-model
  or inaccessible-cardinal assumption is silently substituted for it.
- Historical references are used to motivate the problems. The stated
  equivalences of well-foundedness formulations under PP are not read as
  proofs that PP itself implies any such well-foundedness principle.

2. HISTORICAL FM SECTION: CHECKS PASSED

The section states its ordinary-FM scope, choice ground, faithful/effective
action, external cardinal calculations, and recorded-construction
hypotheses. These qualifications are mathematically important.

The following were directly compared with saved derivations/audits:

- Exact uniform subgroup PP criterion, simultaneous open witness, exact
  stabilizer matching, and the open-core well-orderability test.
- Common compression (C), uniform non-well-orderable profiles, and the
  finite-prototype criterion. The coefficient-vector argument supports
  NDS and finite antichains; it does not realize a nondiscrete example.
- RPC and the conditional sufficiency of (C)+RPC+atom-subset dichotomy:
  RELATIVE_POINT_COMPLETENESS_CRITERION.txt.
- The separate failure of PP and NDS in the multi-HNN example:
  HNN_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_AND_NDS_FAILURE.txt. RPC realization alone is not
  presented as a positive PP model.
- No separable open group under PP and the density/capacity restrictions:
  NO_SEPARABLE_OPEN_GROUP_PP_THEOREM.txt,
  FINITE_PROTOTYPE_LOCAL_CAPACITY_OBSTRUCTION.txt,
  COMMON_COMPRESSION_DENSITY_AND_SUBGROUP_BOUNDS.txt.
  The summary keeps the bound continuum rather than inventing a 2^d(K)
  amplification. Its exclusion of Polish effective topologies is not an
  exclusion of every topology on a countable atom set.
- Fixed-n finite choice: the untouched imported LaTeX section "Sharper
  exclusions for permutation models" explicitly proves, for every fixed
  n>=2, AC_WO+AC_n => AC in ordinary FM via an exact n-cycle of tail
  classes. The new summary states that scope and does not transfer it to
  the pure model W. The OP consequence is consistent with that theorem.
- The historical forcing paragraph explicitly labels its material as
  imported rather than a second independently certified construction.
  The ordinal-target PP / AC_WO equivalence, SVC versus SVC+ distinction,
  and first non-well-orderable powerset-of-an-ordinal statement match the
  relevant opening sections of the imported LaTeX source.

The many field, germ, forest and local-motion examples are concise
retrospective summaries with pointers into the archive. This review did
not re-run every normal-form and scheduling proof. Their explicit
construction hypotheses and distinctions between PP failure, NDS failure
and unclassified NDS are therefore essential and should remain.

3. NEW-MODEL / FRONT-TABLE CONSISTENCY

- The N table asserts AC_WO, DC, failure of AC and DC_(omega_1), old
  cardinal/cofinality preservation, SVC(A), SVC(R), and non-well-orderable
  R, consistently with the detailed section and its proof register.
- A injects into R and A surjects onto R. Neither section infers R
  injects into A from these maps. A^omega approximately R is explicitly
  evaluated in N, whereas the old A^omega approximately A identity is
  evaluated in W.
- All old-set comparisons and relative finite-basis/PP statements remain
  restricted to old sets. They are not promoted to global N axioms.
- The final-segment and nonempty-open-interval cardinality claims reflect
  the resolved anchored-ray result, not the older unresolved tail-square
  bound. No global disjoint-refinement theorem is assumed.
- The extra local separability refinement is explicitly marked as lacking
  a separate saved independent audit, and is not used in the displayed
  main outcomes.
- Native order extendibility in N does not assert global OEP or BPI.
  Global PP, Q(A), FB, NDS, finite cardinal antichains, BPI and R->A remain
  unresolved in the detailed section. The shorter front table makes no
  contradictory claim.
- KWP*_1 / KWP_2 and the failure of ICF are not promoted to KWP_1 or OP.
