DATED LITERATURE AND NOVELTY CHECK
7 October 2026 — preliminary-paper preparation

Bottom-line finding
-------------------
No earlier positive consistency result ZF + not AC + NDS, or ZF + not AC
with the finite-basis conclusion used in our draft, was located in the
primary sources and public searches described below. The latest public
version of Blass–Kulshreshtha still treats cardinal well-foundedness versus
Choice as open. No indexed follow-up to the OpenAI PP release stating our
NDS/finite-antichain/finite-basis corollaries was found.

This supports presenting the draft as additional consequences of the
OpenAI construction. It does NOT establish worldwide priority, exclude
unindexed or unpublished work, or justify calling the underlying model
our construction. Indexing lag is a material limitation for a release
committed on 6 October 2026. The short preliminary paper should avoid
“first”, “previously unknown”, and unconditional global novelty claims.

[OA] OpenAI, The Partition Principle does not imply Choice, manuscript
dated 24 September 2026, pinned public repository commit above.

[BK] Andreas Blass and Dhruv Kulshreshtha, Cardinal Well-foundedness and
Choice, arXiv:2310.09643, v3, 21 August 2026 (v1: 14 October 2023;
v2: 16 January 2024), 14 pages.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09643
https://arxiv.org/html/2310.09643v3
Freshly checked: v3 remains the public current version. Its abstract
and final questions still leave the general WF/AC issue open. Definition

countermodels in the opposite direction. Section 11 remains unchanged.

4. Important older cardinal-lattice prior art
---------------------------------------------
[R78] Arthur L. Rubin, Independence of the axiom of choice from the
greatest lower bound property of cardinals. Preliminary Report.
Notices of the American Mathematical Society 25 (1978), A-14,
abstract 78T-E3; received 16 September 1977.
https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/197801/197801FullIssue.pdf
Downloaded primary issue saved as Rubin1978_Notices.pdf, with extracted
text. Lines 5886–5901 of the local text contain the announcement.
It gives an FM model of ZFU, with uncountably many atoms, the full
symmetric group, and countable supports, satisfying the binary greatest
lower bound property but not AC. It expressly notes a difficulty in
transferring the result to ZF. This is direct prior art, although only a
preliminary abstract rather than a full proof in this source.

[HH76] J. D. Halpern and Paul E. Howard, The law of infinite cardinal
addition is weaker than the axiom of choice, Transactions of the
American Mathematical Society 220 (1976), 195–204.
DOI: 10.1090/S0002-9947-1976-0409183-1.
https://www.ams.org/journals/tran/1976-220-00/S0002-9947-1976-0409183-1/S0002-9947-1976-0409183-1.pdf
Downloaded as HalpernHoward1976.pdf. The opening page p.195 states that
doubling for infinite cardinals implies the corresponding addition-as-
supremum law, and gives the historical independence context. Thus binary
joins without AC are not new. Its footnote already points to Sageev’s
pure ZF result; the article itself constructs a ZFU model.

5. Recommended manuscript wording and limits
---------------------------------------------
Suggested modest formulation:
“We extract a finite-basis property for the injection order from the
stronger structural properties of the recently released OpenAI model.
This yields, in the same model, NDS and the absence of infinite cardinal
antichains, while Choice fails. These consequences are not explicitly
stated in the cited construction. A public literature check completed
on 7 October 2026 did not locate an earlier positive consistency result
of this form.”

Avoid claiming that the search proves priority. Avoid presenting the
classical finite-width theorem, finite-product well-quasi-order argument,
or all-24-forms equivalence as new. Avoid general implications PP⇒NDS
or NDS⇒AC/finite basis. The proof must identify precisely which stronger
OpenAI package it uses. The distinction between full ZF and ordinary
FM/ZFA is essential, particularly for the older lattice examples.
